Nimbo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
App: Nimbo: ADHD Focus Planner (iOS)
Developer: Osman Kartal ("we")
Contact: nimbo.destek@gmail.com

Nimbo is built so that your plans stay yours. Your tasks, notes and day plans live on your device. We do not run servers that store your content, and we have no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you create in Nimbo is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • tasks, steps, routines and day plans
  • the name you choose to be greeted with (optional)
  • energy check-ins, focus sessions, stars and rewards
  • your settings (day start time, notification preferences)

Nimbo does not currently sync this data to iCloud or anywhere else. If we add iCloud sync in the future, we will update this policy before it is turned on. Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or Finder backup.

2. Features that process data on your device only

  • Voice capture: when you dictate a task, speech is turned into text on your device using Apple's on-device transcription. Audio recordings are not saved by Nimbo and are not sent to us.
  • AI suggestions: step suggestions use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your text is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to any AI provider.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: if you use Siri to add tasks, Apple processes the request under its own privacy policy.

Microphone access is requested only when you use voice capture, and you can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Purchases (RevenueCat)

Nimbo Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your payment card or Apple Account details.

To verify purchases and restore Nimbo Pro across your devices, Nimbo uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). Nimbo contacts RevenueCat when the app opens (to check whether you have Pro) and when you buy or restore Pro. RevenueCat receives only:

  • a random, anonymous identifier created by the app (not your name, email or Apple Account)
  • your purchase and subscription transactions (product, dates, status, store country and currency)
  • basic technical data needed to deliver the service (such as app version, iOS version, device model and IP address)
  • your device's app-tracking permission status, which the RevenueCat software reports automatically (Nimbo never asks for tracking permission, so this is always "not determined")

Your tasks, notes, name, energy or mood information are never sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not create an account for you or ask for your email in the app.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your content: delete tasks inside the app (long-press a task › Delete), or delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device.
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: email us at nimbo.destek@gmail.com with the Support ID shown in Nimbo › Pro Center, and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the records linked to it. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its policy.

6. Children

Nimbo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Not medical advice

Nimbo is a planning and focus tool. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional care. You do not need an ADHD diagnosis to use it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because almost all data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For anything else, contact nimbo.destek@gmail.com. Users in Türkiye can read the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
